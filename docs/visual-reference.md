# KOPDES — active visual reference

The project adapts **page composition** from an archived 1 September 2026 desktop screenshot of `lphishiring.com`. The live website was not consistently inspectable. Original-site mobile and below-fold behavior are unknown; 100% pixel parity must not be asserted.

At an approximately 1000×563 desktop viewport, the reference has a geometric band, photographic masthead, four-tab dark navigation, warm welcome, **portrait left**, message/heading **right**, and a compact media panel directly below. See `docs/reference-geometry.md` and `docs/layout-calibration-1000.md` for estimates and tests.

KOPDES substitutes its own identity, red `#B91C1C`, deep teal `#065366`, cream `#FFF0CF`, Indonesian text, a labeled documentary Prabowo portrait and photographs of named cooperatives. The portrait is not a quotation or endorsement. No entertainment artwork, fictional service, official claim or restaurant text may be copied.

The site retains eight routes. `/daftar/` contains guidance only and does not transmit NIK. Image authors, licenses and local transformations are documented in `docs/assets-credits.md`. Earlier notes describing a right-side portrait, decorative disc, fictional addresses and unsafe WhatsApp form are historical and superseded.
